Methods summary

The 2D and 3D contactomes were derived from the same C. elegans nerve ring specimen (L2 animal, ~23 hours post hatch). The 2D contactome was generated from a SEM-based adjacency matrix and the 3D contactome from a volumetric segmentation of the same dataset. Spearman rank correlation was computed per neuron across all contact partners detected by either method, with absent contacts scored as zero. Non-neuronal cells (body wall muscle, excretory gland, fragment reconstructions, CAN neurons) were excluded from all analyses.
